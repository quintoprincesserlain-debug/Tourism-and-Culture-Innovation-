INNOVATION SECTORS

Tourism + Culture

Connecting travelers with authentic local stories while helping communities protect their heritage.

Explore the concept

1. The Challenge

Local cultural experiences can be difficult to discover online. Small tourism operators and cultural workers may have limited marketing resources, while popular destinations can experience overcrowding and insufficient visitor awareness.

2. The Innovation

Discover

Find heritage sites, local guides, festivals, crafts, and community-led tours.

Learn

Read short cultural stories and use QR codes to access reliable site information.

Travel Responsibly

View local etiquette, accessibility details, transport options, and low-impact travel tips.

3. Target Users

  • Domestic and international travelers
  • Local tourism enterprises and guides
  • Artists, artisans, and cultural organizations
  • Local governments and heritage managers

4. Expected Impact

The project aims to increase visibility for local culture, create income opportunities, improve visitor education, and encourage responsible tourism. Community members should help decide how their culture is represented and what information is shared.

ReachPlatform visits and listing views
LivelihoodInquiries and bookings for local providers
ProtectionCommunity feedback and conservation indicators

5. Implementation

  1. Consult residents, cultural practitioners, and tourism offices.
  2. Build a small prototype featuring a few verified destinations.
  3. Test it with visitors and local providers.
  4. Improve accessibility, accuracy, and privacy protections.
  5. Measure results and expand only with community support.
