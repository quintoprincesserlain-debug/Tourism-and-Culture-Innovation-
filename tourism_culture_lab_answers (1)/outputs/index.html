Tourism & Culture

Digital discovery for meaningful travel, thriving communities, and protected heritage.

The proposal: CultureConnect

CultureConnect is a proposed digital guide that helps visitors discover verified local cultural experiences and helps communities share their heritage on their own terms.

Core features

Discover local stories

Explore heritage places, festivals, food, crafts, and community-led tours.

Travel responsibly

Read local etiquette, accessibility details, and low-impact travel tips.

Support communities

Find local guides and cultural enterprises and contact providers directly.

How success will be measured

  • Participation and feedback from local providers
  • Visitor satisfaction and cultural learning
  • Inquiries and bookings for community businesses
  • Accuracy, accessibility, and heritage-protection indicators

Implementation plan

  1. Consult community stakeholders.
  2. Create a small prototype with verified listings.
  3. Test with residents, providers, and visitors.
  4. Improve the product and measure its impact.

Innovation should serve people, place, and heritage.
